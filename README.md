
## Final Policy Recommendation

The final policy audit compares a global fraud threshold with
segment-specific thresholds under different alert-capacity and
business-cost assumptions.

### Key findings

- The global threshold generated 20,852 alerts on the historical test set.
- The segment-specific policy generated 7,104 alerts.
- The global policy missed 0 labeled fraud cases in this test period.
- The segment-specific policy missed 26 labeled fraud cases.
- Estimated costs depend on illustrative assumptions, not verified bank economics.

**Important:** These are historical test results, not guarantees of future performance or proof of production readiness.
